Scams and Scammers

A comprehensive resource on various types of scams and phishing attempts, including fake compensation offers, inheritance scams, and phishing emails designed to steal personal and financial information. It highlights common red flags and provides warnings about scams involving organizations like the FBI, Interpol, and the IMF, as well as fake bank websites and phishing emails mimicking legitimate services such as Netflix, Microsoft 365, and Binance. Included are specific examples of scam emails and advice on how to identify and avoid them. Additionally details of spam and persistent spammer behavior, offering insights into how to deal with such threats.
